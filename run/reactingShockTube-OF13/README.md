Verification case for multi-species reactive shock tube as performed in the paper of Ferrer P.J.M., Buttay R., Lehnasch G., Mura A. - A detailed verification procedure for compressible reactive multicomponent Navier–Stokes solvers - 2014 (DOI:10.1016/j.compfluid.2013.10.014).

Following the paper, we consider a 9 species with 18 elementary steps chemical mechanism, the details of which are provided in Kee R.J., Grcar J.F., Smooke M.D., Miller J.A., Meeks E. - PREMIX A FORTRAN Program for Modeling Steady Laminar One-Dimensional Premixed Flames - 1998.

The current case is the modified version of one created by Pablo Kandel in his project for the course "CFD with OpenSource Software" at the Chalmers University of Technology (see Proceedings of CFD with OpenSource Software, 2024, Edited by Nilsson H. https://dx.doi.org/10.17196/OS_CFD#YEAR_2024).
